# Storage on your device

StudyFlow does not use cookies. It does keep a few things in your browser's own storage so the app works. Nothing here is used for tracking or advertising, and we do not show a cookie banner because each item is needed to give you the service you asked for.

## What is kept

- **Your plan** (assignments, timetable, grades, notes, timer) under the name `studyflow_storage_v1`, so it shows instantly and works offline. Removed when you sign out.
- **Your choices** that survive signing out: light or dark theme and whether the sidebar is shrunk. These are not personal data about your studies.
- **Your age answer** (the band you picked, not a date of birth), until you sign out, so we do not ask again straight away.
- **PDFs you attach to notes**, in the browser's IndexedDB. They never leave your device. Removed when you sign out.
- **A copy of your account's data** that Firebase keeps in IndexedDB so the app works offline. Removed when you sign out.
- **Sign-in information** that Firebase keeps in your browser so you stay signed in.
- **Short-lived notes** during sign-in (for example, that a sign-in is in progress).

## Removing it

*Sign out* clears your plan, the offline copy and your PDFs from this device. You can also clear your browser's site data.

See also [Privacy](/privacy).
