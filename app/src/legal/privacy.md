# Privacy

StudyFlow is a study planner. This page explains, in plain words, what it keeps about you, where, and how to remove it.

## What we keep

- **If you sign in with Google:** your name, email address and profile picture address, as Google gives them to us.
- **What you type:** your profile (name, university, programme, semester, time zone, and a picture if you choose one), assignments, timetable classes, grades, notes, timer settings and finished focus sessions.
- **Your age band:** whether you said you are 13 to 17 or 18 and over, and whether a parent or guardian agreed. We do not ask for your date of birth.
- **Guests too:** if you use StudyFlow without signing in, your plan is still saved to a private guest account so it appears on that device. If you clear your browser, you lose access to it.
- **PDFs you attach to notes stay on your device.** They are never uploaded.

## What we do not do

- No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no selling or sharing of your data.
- No profiling and no messages to you. We only use your data to show you your own plan.

## Where it is kept, and who handles it

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Firestore) stores your account and your plan. Only your own account can read it; this is enforced by security rules that are tested automatically. The project owner can see it through the Firebase console.
- **Vercel** hosts the website and keeps ordinary server logs (such as your IP address, browser and the time).
- Both are companies outside Kenya, so your data is processed abroad. Data location: to be confirmed by the project owner before launch.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can delete your account and all its data yourself (see below). Guest data that is never deleted may stay stored until we clean it up; we have no automatic clean-up yet.

## Your choices

- **See and take a copy:** *Your account* then *Download my data* saves everything as one file.
- **Correct it:** *Edit profile*, or change any item in the app.
- **Delete it:** *Your account* then *Delete my account* removes your plan, your profile and your sign-in account, and clears this device. It cannot be undone.
- **Questions or requests:** contact the project owner. Contact address: to be added before launch.
