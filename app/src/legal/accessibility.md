# Accessibility

We want every student to be able to use StudyFlow, including with a keyboard, a screen reader, or a small phone.

## What we do

- Every screen is checked automatically for accessibility problems against the WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines, in light and dark mode, on every change.
- Buttons and links on a phone are at least 44 pixels tall so they are easy to tap.
- You can use the app with a keyboard, and there is a "Skip to content" link at the top.
- Colours have enough contrast, and animations stop if your device is set to reduce motion.
- Messages that appear (saved, offline, errors) are announced to screen readers.

## What we cannot promise

Automatic checks find many problems, but not all. We have not had StudyFlow tested by disabled students or an accessibility auditor yet, so there may be problems we have not found.
