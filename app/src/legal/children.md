# Children and parents

StudyFlow is made for students. Because some students are under 18, here is how we treat young users.

## Ages

- **Under 13:** you cannot create or link an account. You can still use StudyFlow as a guest, on your own device, without giving us a name or email.
- **13 to 17:** you can create an account if a **parent or guardian knows and agrees**. We ask you to confirm this before you sign in. We do not check it, so please be honest. Parents and guardians: you can ask us to delete your child's data at any time.
- **18 and over:** no extra step.

## What we collect from young users

The same as from everyone, and no more: the name, email and picture from their Google account, and what they type into the planner. No date of birth, no phone number, no school records, no location. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is shared with anyone.

## Parents and guardians

- Your child can see and download everything we hold, and can delete the account themselves from *Your account*.
- To ask us to delete data or to ask a question, contact the project owner. Contact address: to be added before launch.
- If you do not agree with your child having an account, ask them to use *Delete my account*, or contact us.

## Why we ask only for an age band

We want to know enough to treat young users carefully without collecting more personal information. The age question is a self-declaration: it is a protection for honest users, not a check.

See also [Privacy](/privacy).
